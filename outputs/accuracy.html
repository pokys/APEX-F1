"Typical probability" is the geometric mean of the probability given to the actual winner (equivalent to the mean log loss); it rewards confident correct calls and punishes confident wrong ones. The backtest uses the noise and blend settings tuned on this same season, so it is slightly optimistic; the live table has no such advantage.
