How to read this page
Order of the table
Drivers are listed in the predicted finishing order: by the median simulated position (the position the driver reaches or beats in half of the simulations), ties broken by the win/pole chance.
Bars and percentages
Win / podium (race, sprint) or pole / front row / top 10 (qualifying): the share of simulations in which that happens. Bright part = the headline result, darker parts = the wider ones.
▲ / ▼ next to a driver
Places the driver is predicted to gain (green ▲) or lose (red ▼): their place in the predicted order (the # column) against the last time they were classified – the starting grid for a sprint or race (penalties included), otherwise the latest session of this weekend (e.g. FP1 or the sprint). – means no change.
Predicted
Median finishing position. Hover it for the mean; the mean is pulled down by unlikely outcomes, so the median reads better.
Driver detail (click the name / "Detail")
Start → median and most likely position, DNF risk, the driver's results in this weekend's sessions, the win/pole chance before the latest session and now (e.g. "Win chance 17% before FP1+SQ → 6% now": how much the latest sessions changed the prediction), and the chance of finishing in each position.
Badges
Red: grid penalty from the FIA stewards' decisions or race control (−N grid, pit start, back of grid). Blue outline: substitute driver from the official entry list.
Dry / Wet / Mix
The model simulates a dry and a wet race. Mix weights them by the chance that the session runs in the wet, estimated from the Open-Meteo forecast (rain probability, expected amount and weather code). Weather icons: ☀️/🌙 dry, ⛅ mostly dry, 🌦️ showers possible, 🌧️ wet likely, ⛈️ thunderstorm; click an icon for the numbers.
Weekend Delta
How much this weekend's sessions moved the driver's rating up or down.
Accuracy
"How accurate is it?" compares past predictions with the results and with a simple guess based on the championship order.
